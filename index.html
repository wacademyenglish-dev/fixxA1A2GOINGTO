STRATEGY FRAMEWORK 1

Grammar: Be Going To (Predictions)

PREDICTIONS WITH EVIDENCE

We use Verb To Be + going to to predict the future when we can SEE evidence right now.

"Look at those black clouds! It is going to rain."


"He has a microphone. He is going to sing."
